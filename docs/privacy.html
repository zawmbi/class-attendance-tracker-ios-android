Privacy Policy

Attendize · Last updated June 8, 2026

Attendize (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps you track class attendance. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. We’ve tried to keep it plain.

  • Your classes and attendance records are stored locally on your device — we do not upload or store them on our servers.
  • If you create an account, your email and name are handled by our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication) solely to sign you in.
  • Location (if you allow it) is used on your device to remind you when you’re near class. It is not collected, stored, or shared by us.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.
  • You can delete your account and all data anytime in the app (Settings → Delete account).

Information we handle

Account information. If you sign up or sign in (including with Google or Apple), we receive your email address and display name through Google Firebase Authentication in order to create and secure your account. We do not receive your password.

Class and attendance data. The classes you add and the attendance you log are stored locally on your device. This information stays on your device unless you choose to remove it.

Location data. With your permission (“While Using the App”), the app may use your location to provide proximity-based class reminders. This processing happens on your device; we do not collect or transmit your location.

Diagnostics. We do not collect analytics or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To provide app features you request (tracking, reminders, forecasts).
  • We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or cross-app/website tracking.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Authentication — handles sign-in. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Apple (if used) — Apple Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Account data persists until you delete your account. You can delete your account and erase on-device data anytime via Settings → Delete account, or wipe local data via Settings → Clear all data & start fresh. Deleting the app also removes locally stored data.

Children’s privacy

Attendize is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly. Material changes will be reflected here.
